---
title: Drills
description: The roleplay scenarios, difficulty levels, and persona filters.
---

A **drill** is the scenario your rep practices. You pass its key as `drillKey` to
`AthosRoleplay.create({ drillKey })`. An unknown key returns `DRILL_NOT_FOUND`.

## Available drills

Keys are `<line>-<practice>`: `ma` Medicare Advantage, `ms` Medicare Supplement, `fe` Final
Expense, `u65` under-65 private health, `hi` Hospital Indemnity, `ci` Critical Illness.

### Medicare Advantage

| `drillKey` | Scenario | Starts | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ma-full-sale` | **Full enrollment.** A complete Medicare Advantage enrollment call, intro to close. Scored for skill and for compliance on the default Medicare rubric (yes / partial / no per question, by category; calls of 5+ min) — see [`complianceScorecard`](/backend/reading-calls#the-score-object). | Customer calls in | Through enrollment |
| `ma-t65` | **Turning 65.** Enroll a beneficiary going onto Medicare for the first time — they have never had a plan and will ask the rep to explain everything. Same enrollment ground and compliance scoring (5+ min) as `ma-full-sale`. | Customer calls in | Through enrollment |
| `ma-objection-handling` | **Objection handling.** The early minutes of an inbound call with a skeptical, resistant caller. The rep has to build trust, clear the pushback, and move the call forward; no enrollment. | Customer calls in | Opening 3–5 min only |
| `ma-needs-analysis` | **Needs analysis.** Discovery only: learn the caller's situation, doctors, prescriptions and what they want. The caller resists if the rep jumps ahead to presenting or enrolling. | Customer calls in | Discovery only |
| `ma-sep-hunting` | **SEP hunting.** Find out whether the beneficiary qualifies for a Special Enrollment Period by asking the right questions. The persona never volunteers the qualifying event — the rep has to uncover it. | Mid-call — intro done; name, ZIP and Medicare number already given | Discovery only |
| `ma-hhc` | **Home Health Care — full sale.** The whole call: take a vague inbound enquiry, uncover the need, and enroll the beneficiary in home health care. | Customer calls in | Through enrollment |
| `ma-hhc-downsell` | **Home Health Care — downsell.** Pivot to home health care when there is no Medicare Advantage sale to be made. | Mid-call — just told there is no better plan for them | Through enrollment |
| `ma-hhc-crosssell` | **Home Health Care — cross-sell.** The client thinks the call is over; open a separate home-health policy without it sounding like part of the plan just enrolled. | Mid-call — Medicare Advantage application just submitted | Through enrollment |
| `ma-hhc-upsell` | **Home Health Care — upsell.** A happy client with no reason to buy anything. The rep has to create the opening; the client raises nothing. | Rep calls an existing client for a routine check-in | Through enrollment |

### Medicare Supplement

| `drillKey` | Scenario | Starts | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ms-full-sale` | **Full sale.** A beneficiary who already has a Medigap plan and wants a lower premium, not Medicare Advantage. Shop their plan against other carriers and handle medical underwriting; if they can't qualify, pivot to an ancillary policy. | Customer calls in | Through enrollment |

### Final Expense

| `drillKey` | Scenario | Starts | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fe-full-sale` | **Full sale.** A complete final-expense call, intro to close: discovery, health questions, quote, application and verification. | Customer calls in | Through enrollment |
| `fe-objection-handling` | **Objection handling.** The cold open of a Final Expense call: the early objections a caller raises before qualifying starts, such as confusion about the ad, reluctance to answer questions, and cost worries. | Customer calls in | Opening 3–5 min only |
| `fe-closing` | **Closing.** The closing half of a Final Expense sale — price bracketing, waiting-period explanation, SSN/bank collection, and verification recovery. | Mid-call — intro and health questions done; the prospect asks to hear plans and prices | Through enrollment |

### Under-65 private health

| `drillKey` | Scenario | Starts | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `u65-full-sale` | **Full sale.** A self-employed or small-business prospect under 65 asked online about health coverage. Qualify them, present a private plan (or an ACA marketplace plan if they don't qualify), and close. | Rep calls a prospect who filled out an online form | Through enrollment |

### Hospital Indemnity

| `drillKey` | Scenario | Starts | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `hi-downsell` | **Downsell.** Pivot to hospital indemnity when there is no Medicare Advantage sale to be made. | Mid-call — just told there is no better Medicare Advantage plan today | Through enrollment |
| `hi-upsell` | **Upsell.** A happy client on a routine check-in. The rep has to create the opening for hospital indemnity; the client raises nothing. | Rep calls an existing client for a routine check-in | Through enrollment |
| `hi-crosssell` | **Cross-sell.** Introduce hospital indemnity while walking a client through their new Medicare Advantage plan. | Mid-call — enrollment underway; plan picked, about to walk the Summary of Benefits | Through enrollment |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

<Callout type="info">
  Keys are **never renamed**, and a newly added key works without an SDK upgrade. A key is withdrawn
  only if it turns out to misdescribe its scenario; a withdrawn key returns `DRILL_NOT_FOUND`.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **Four drills start mid-call.** `fe-closing`, `ma-hhc-downsell`, `ma-hhc-crosssell` and
  `ma-hhc-upsell` open with the persona already at the moment the scenario is about — its first
  line is a reaction to what has just happened, not a greeting. Tell the rep the setup before
  they connect; the persona will not explain it.
</Callout>

### Typed keys in the SDK (opt-in)

`drillKey` accepts any `string`, so newly added drills work without an SDK upgrade. For editor
autocomplete and compile-time checking, the SDK exports the known catalog — use it where your
keys are static, or render a scenario picker from the runtime list:

```ts
import { ATHOS_DRILL_KEYS, type AthosDrillKey } from '@useathos/sdk';

ATHOS_DRILL_KEYS.forEach((key) => addOption(key)); // ['ma-full-sale', 'ma-t65', …, 'ci-crosssell']
const drillKey: AthosDrillKey = 'fe-closing';      // opt-in compile-time check
```

## Difficulty

Pass `difficulty` to set how challenging the persona is:

| Value | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `Beginner` | More cooperative persona, simpler objections. |
| `Advanced` | Standard difficulty. **Default** when `difficulty` is omitted. |
| `Elite` | Tougher persona, harder objections. |

```ts
AthosRoleplay.create({ token, drillKey: 'ma-full-sale', difficulty: 'Elite' });
```

## Persona filters

`filters` narrows which persona is chosen. It is **best-effort**: `state` is honored whenever a
persona in that state is available for the drill, and when none is, Athos serves an unfiltered
persona instead of failing the call.

| Filter | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `state` | Prefer a persona located in a given US state (e.g. `"CA"`). Dropped when the drill has no persona in that state. |
| `category` | Reserved for future use; accepted but not applied in v1. |

```ts
AthosRoleplay.create({
  token,
  drillKey: 'ma-full-sale',
  filters: { state: 'CA' },
});
```

<Callout type="info">
  A filter never fails a call — at worst it is ignored. A drill whose persona pool is entirely
  unavailable is a different situation: the session returns `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` (503), which is
  temporary, not a filter error. It does consume the session token, so recover by minting a new
  token rather than replaying the same request. If you need hard guarantees about which personas
  are available, talk to your Athos contact.
</Callout>
